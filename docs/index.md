<section id="about">
  <h2>What is this? · ما هذه الخزنة؟</h2>
  <p><strong>EN:</strong> BENBENHUB-CORE is the <strong>Single Source of Truth</strong> for BENBENHUB architecture — an Obsidian vault that defines how a <em>hidden parent</em> governs three <strong>sovereign platforms</strong> (Circuit Rack, Circuit Pro, Circuit Labs) with backend-only integration, platform isolation, and three governance gates. Engineers, Maestro, and AI collaborators use it <strong>before</strong> designing features or shipping code.</p>
  <p class="muted" dir="rtl"><strong>AR:</strong> BENBENHUB-CORE هي مصدر الحقيقة الواحد للمعمارية — خزنة Obsidian تحدد كيف يحكم <em>أب مخفي</em> ثلاث منصات سيادية عبر تكامل خلفي فقط وعزل المنصات وثلاث بوابات حوكمة.</p>
</section>

<section id="principles">
  <h2>Core principles · المبادئ</h2>
  <h3>Hidden parent · الأب المخفي</h3>
  <p>BENBENHUB sets internal strategy and law — not a public consumer umbrella or unified marketplace app.</p>
  <h3>Platform isolation · عزل المنصات</h3>
  <p>Separate UI, schema, deploy, and failure domain per island. No cross-schema DB access, no shared consumer shell, no iframes.</p>
  <table>
    <thead><tr><th>SSOT domain</th><th>Owner</th></tr></thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><td>Products &amp; orders</td><td>Circuit Rack</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Profile &amp; verification</td><td>Circuit Pro</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Content &amp; courses</td><td>Circuit Labs</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Auth &amp; wallet</td><td>Shared core (gated)</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
  <h3>Governance gates · بوابات الحوكمة</h3>
  <table>
    <thead><tr><th>Gate</th><th>EN</th><th>AR</th></tr></thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><td><strong>Truth</strong></td><td>Reality documented before work closes</td><td>توثيق الواقع قبل الإغلاق</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Immutability</strong></td><td>Decisions superseded formally</td><td>استبدال القرارات رسمياً</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Scope</strong></td><td>Work stays within isolation rules</td><td>العمل ضمن العزل</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
  <p class="muted">Cross-platform integration: <strong>APIs, events, and stable IDs only</strong>.</p>
